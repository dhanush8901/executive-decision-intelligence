Executive Decision Intelligence

Problem

Hundreds of KPIs, but executives cannot tell which ones actually explain performance. Link operational KPIs to financial outcomes and generate evidence-based insights with an LLM.

Algorithm flow

InputProcessDecisionOutput
Collect KPI time series from all departments + financial outcomes (revenue, margin)
Align to common period; handle missing values; detrend
Compute lagged correlation / Granger-style test of each KPI vs outcome
Fit regularised model (Lasso) to select few real drivers
Driver significant & stable across periods?
YES → keep as 'key driver'; NO → drop as noise
LLM turns drivers + recent movement into an executive narrative
Top-driver dashboard + plain-language insight
